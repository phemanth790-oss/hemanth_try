The Man Behind The Gloves

Mahendra Singh Dhoni, commonly known as MS Dhoni or Mahi, is an Indian professional cricketer who captained the Indian national team in limited-overs formats from 2007 to 2017 and in Test cricket from 2008 to 2014.


He is widely regarded as one of the greatest cricket captains and wicketkeeper-batsmen of all time. Known for his calm demeanor under pressure ("Captain Cool"), he is the only captain in history to win all three major ICC trophies: the T20 World Cup (2007), the ODI World Cup (2011), and the Champions Trophy (2013).
